## Publications

["Hawkish or Dovish? Inferring Intended Monetary Policy from the Fed's Dot Plot."](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0165188926000400) Manuel González-Astudillo and Rakeen Tanvir. *Journal of Economic Dynamics and Control*, 2026.

We use the Federal Reserve's "dot plot"—the Summary of Economic Projections—to gauge policymakers' intended monetary-policy responses to inflation and economic activity in real time.

## Working Papers

"The Cost of Flexibility: Informal Governance and Resilience in the South African Taxi Industry." Christopher F. Eaglin and Rakeen Tanvir. Revise and resubmit at the *Strategic Management Journal*, 2026.

We study how the management of informal institutions shapes small-firm performance in South Africa's minibus taxi industry. Using administrative loan records, high-frequency GPS data, and weather shocks, we find that organizational flexibility increases vehicle utilization and improves loan repayment, though it does not fully protect firms when severe shocks occur.
